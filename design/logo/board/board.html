What we heard

The brief

A Doha agency for restaurants and companies: luxury bilingual websites, SEO, social media, content and photography. The name Athar means the trace or mark something leaves behind. The identity must feel luxurious, modern and Arabic-first, work on ink and ivory backgrounds, and survive a 16 px favicon and a shop-front sign.

luxurymodernpreciseArabic-first

Success: unmistakably Athar on its own, legible at 16 px, one colour and reversed, distinct from the gradient-blob and lowercase-sans agency look.

Concept A

Kufi monogram

أثر built in square Kufi on a nine-unit grid; the three dots of ث become three pixels, the only elements lifted off the letters.

  • The name is the mark: it works as symbol and wordmark at once
  • Square Kufi reads as heritage and modern together, right for a luxury Arabic-first agency
  • Brass letters with ivory pixels on ink: two colours, one idea, scales to a favicon
  • Strict grid means it embroiders, engraves and animates cleanly

Recommendation & next steps

Our recommendation

  • Install on the website header, footer, favicon and social preview
  • Register the mark: trademark search and filing in Qatar
  • Extend into a pattern and social templates from the nine-unit grid
